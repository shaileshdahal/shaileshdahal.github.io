My research develops machine learning methods for biomedical and clinical data, with an emphasis on generative modeling, representation learning, healthcare informatics, and structured biomedical knowledge.

## Generative Machine Learning

I study generative models for learning from complex and incomplete data, including diffusion models, flow matching, representation learning, and language models.

## Healthcare Informatics

I develop methods for electronic health records and longitudinal clinical data, including informative missingness, risk prediction, and patient trajectory modeling.

## Biomedical NLP & Structured Knowledge

I work on biomedical language and knowledge representation, including biomedical text generation, ontology-oriented modeling, and **taxonomy completion**.
