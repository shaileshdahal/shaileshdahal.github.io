I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the **University of Iowa**, working at the intersection of **machine learning, healthcare informatics, and biomedical AI**.

My research focuses on **generative models and representation learning** for biomedical and clinical data, including electronic health records, patient trajectories, biomedical language, and structured knowledge such as **taxonomies and ontologies**.

## Research

- **Generative Machine Learning** — generative models, diffusion, flow matching, representation learning, and language models
- **Healthcare Informatics** — electronic health records, incomplete clinical data, risk prediction, and patient trajectories
- **Biomedical NLP & Knowledge** — biomedical language modeling, taxonomy completion, ontologies, and structured biomedical knowledge
